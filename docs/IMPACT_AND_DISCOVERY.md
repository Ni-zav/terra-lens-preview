# Why Terra-Lens could matter — and what it has not proved

**2026 NASA Space Apps / award development branch.** This is an evidence-grounded *product hypothesis*, not a record of measured adoption or validated climate interventions.

## Human problem, not just a charting problem

NASA already makes Earth-system observations and models accessible. But a public claim like “one place is warming faster than another” requires identifying equivalent units and periods, retaining valid monthly data, aggregating years, testing the *difference* (not merely two separate p-values), and explaining spatial/model uncertainty. A misleading chart is fast to share; a defensible finding often is not.

**Our promise is deliberately narrower:** Make it easier for non-specialists to investigate an environmental question, inspect why a difference may or may not be supported, and hand off a reproducible evidence file to someone qualified to validate it.

## Who benefits, which question, which defensible next action?

| Potential user | User question | Data Terra-Lens can supply | Responsible next step | Not justified by this app |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| School / university educator | Do two apparently different warming slopes really differ? | Monthly reanalysis point values; aligned annual B−A HAC comparison; uncertainty | Teach sampling and hypothesis testing; verify with source data | Proving unequal local climate change, or a causal lesson |
| Citizen scientist / science communicator | What would change my mind about an environmental headline? | Named source URLs, download of actual valid months, incomplete-year exclusion and confidence intervals | Publish a claim with location, units, time range and caveats | “NASA proved” a sweeping city-wide trend |
| Community research group | Which sampled places merit field-station follow-up? | Fixed-frame multi-location slope screen, failed request accounting, selected A/B audit | Select candidates for independent station validation | Exposure-weighted heat risk, hazard alerts or funding allocations |
| Agricultural / water research educator | Is modeled daily precipitation rate trending at our sample point? | PRECTOTCORR mm/day averages, annual means, time series | Corroborate with rain gauges and crop/soil/hydrology observations | Annual rainfall accumulation, drought, crop failure or flood prediction |

A prototype showing a scientifically honest *non-finding* is valuable: our earlier 2001–2024 Jakarta vs Yogyakarta **paired** result does not establish unequal fitted warming rates under the exploratory HAC approximation. The workflow should teach users **not** to convert two significant-looking individual trends into a false comparative assertion.

## Three horizons of value

**Today — education and evidence communication:** A browser-only investigation with real NASA data, exact units, transparent losses and an auditable scientific notebook/JSON. The immediate observable output is an evidence package, not an intervention.

**Next — targeted local validation:** Schools, community researchers or agencies could use the predeclared screen to choose where to examine independent instruments and exposure indicators. This benefit requires user interviews and independent validation; none have been claimed.

**Later — broader Earth-system context:** An expanded product could combine correctly quality-controlled NASA MODIS/VIIRS/FIRMS fire *detection*, MODIS/AppEEARS land vegetation, GPM precipitation, GLDAS modeled soil moisture and properly supported spatial regions. Each requires product-specific QA/temporal supports, licensing, acquisition, uncertainty, scientific validation and resource cost. FIRMS fire hotspots are **detections, not burned-area estimates**; rainfall trends are not direct flood or drought impact.

## Best Use of Data positioning

NASA describes the category as the project that best makes space data accessible or leverages it for a unique application. Terra-Lens's candidate distinction is **auditable, user-guided comparative claim testing that resists false certainty**. Its novelty is not a new Earth observation, not a novel regression formula, and not displaying a satellite-themed globe.

The original finder queries NASA POWER point data; the Giovanni tab separately analyzes an authentic area-average export from the challenge-listed service. See [the source handoff](NASA_QUALIFYING_SOURCE_HANDOFF.md) for provenance and limitations. Import capability alone is not proof that any user-supplied file is authentic.

## Honest impact metrics to measure, not invent

A 10-minute task test with 3–5 participants could measure: (a) did they identify source, units and time support, (b) could they distinguish separate p-values from a direct B−A comparison, (c) did they reach a justified next step without a fabricated risk conclusion, (d) could someone else reproduce their finding from the export, and (e) how many data/API errors were surfaced instead of hidden. Record actual counts and direct quotes only after testing. No adoption, time-saved, lives-saved, improved crop yield, emissions reduced, forecasting accuracy or public-sector deployment is presently verified.

## One sentence for a judge

**“Terra-Lens helps people turn an Earth-data headline into a falsifiable, shareable scientific investigation—and tells them when the evidence is not strong enough for action.”**

We should only use this sentence in a competition demo if the corresponding live functions and NASA source gates have been validated, not merely designed.
