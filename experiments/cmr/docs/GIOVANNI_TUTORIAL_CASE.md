# NASA-published Giovanni tutorial table: candidate source analysis

NASA's GES DISC [tutorial notebook](https://github.com/nasa/gesdisc-tutorials/blob/f81701924c0336811ef3b90e659ffeb83c28de1b/news_%26_dia/Giovanni_MERRA2_Extreme_Monthly.ipynb) says its [CSV](https://github.com/nasa/gesdisc-tutorials/blob/f81701924c0336811ef3b90e659ffeb83c28de1b/news_%26_dia/data/g4.ints.M2SMNXEDI_2_HWF.19800101-20231231.JJA.csv) was generated by Giovanni and identifies it as MERRA-2 monthly extremes detection indices **M2SMNXEDI version 2**, HWF, June–August, 1980–2023. The tutorial calls the values country means for Canada and the United States and describes HWF as a count per month. Its data collection DOI is [10.5067/O8AX56DO60MI](https://doi.org/10.5067/O8AX56DO60MI); the baseline is 1991–2020. NASA's [collection catalog](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/collections.json?short_name=M2SMNXEDI&version=2) identifies the global model-derived collection. [NASA scientific documentation](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20220017884/downloads/agu_poster.pdf) defines HWF as days meeting the heatwave criterion, not the number of distinct heatwaves.

The checked-in file is the exact 468-byte NASA-published tutorial CSV, including its UTF-8 BOM and line endings, retrieved 15 November 2026. SHA-256: `34193c502c6417eb1163967a33dc2b19a87ea8be5518142f6196cb77b0a2b8fd`. Source commit: `f81701924c0336811ef3b90e659ffeb83c28de1b`. Its 44 rows contain one reported number per country per JJA season; there are no missing years or explicit fill values in this file. It is an already-aggregated seasonal table, **not** 44 years of raw monthly granules and must not be processed as such.

For an exploratory descriptive check, regress the published numbers on actual year. JavaScript `linearTrend` gives Canada +0.190275 and United States +0.390416 tutorial count units per decade. `python scripts/verify-giovanni-tutorial.py` hashes the same bytes and independently checks both slopes with SciPy. Do not infer a significant difference between these two slopes, a causal mechanism, population exposure, or a forecast. The period, locations and source were selected after reviewing NASA's example. Serial dependence, selection, model error and processing uncertainty are not accounted for.

## Provenance and quality gaps

NASA's notebook states the table came from Giovanni, but the published CSV has no source job URL, processing history, spatial weighting or per-observation quality flags. The integer-valued output does not disclose rounding or precision. Neither the exact geographic mask nor the handling of incomplete spatial coverage can be independently reconstructed from the CSV alone. Therefore, this is a **NASA-published candidate example**, not a fully verified original Giovanni job/export or a claim that the strict source and quality gate has passed. It should not be presented as a decision-ready physical estimate. Consult the product documentation and obtain a source export with its lineage and quality metadata before certifying that gate.
